# paints.json

Palette data for the panel's Palettes tab, as in the 2D app (multiplayer-canvas):

- `builtin`: 78 Golden Heavy Body acrylics, from the spectral reflectance Golden Artist Colors
  gave Andrew Glassner and Eric Haines with permission to share
  (https://www.realtimerendering.com/golden.html), seen under D65 and converted to sRGB; and
  three limited palettes made of them (Zorn, Split primary, Earth).
- `library`: paint colors by medium and brand line from artistpigments.org
  (https://artistpigments.org), licensed **CC BY-NC 4.0**
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/): unlike the rest of vdraw (MIT), this part
  may not be used commercially.
